Description: Computational Suite for Experimental and Analytical Portland Cement Concrete Proportioning via the IPT/EPUSP Method (Helene & Terzian) in C++23 and Qt 6.12.0
"High-precision rational mix design in IEEE 754 double precision, binary coarse aggregate packing optimization, Abrams/Lyse/Molinari regressions, 4-Quadrant Dosage Diagram, and ABNT NBR normative compliance."

Concrete Mix Designer is a free native desktop Civil Engineering and Concrete Technology application of an educational, academic, and auxiliary nature designed for study, research, and result comparison by students, professors, researchers, concrete technologists, and civil engineers. It is built entirely in modern C++23 with a Qt 6.12.0 GUI (Widgets, Charts, and Sql), an embedded in-memory SQLite 3 relational standards database (:memory:), and a standalone command-line interface (concrete-mix-designer-cli.exe).

The application integrates four essential pillars into a cohesive, transparent, and sovereign workflow:

1. Structural Parameters & Embedded Normative Database (ABNT NBR):
Definition of characteristic compressive strength (fck from 10 to 100 MPa), preparation condition (Conditions A, B, and C per ABNT NBR 12655:2022 with automatic enforcement blocking fck > 20 MPa in Condition B and fck > 15 MPa in Condition C, historical known standard deviation with n >= 20, or Laboratory/Arbitrary mode), target dosage strength calculation (fcj = fck + 1.65 * Sd), slump consistency classes (S10 to S220 per ABNT NBR 8953:2015), Environmental Aggressiveness Classes (CAA I to IV), nominal reinforcement cover c_nom (Reinforced Concrete RC and Prestressed Concrete PC +5 mm per ABNT NBR 6118:2023 Table 7.2), geometric compatibility check D_max <= 1.2 * c_nom, and ready-mix batching weighing tolerances (ABNT NBR 7212:2025 Section 5.5).

2. Material Characterization, Binary Coarse Blend & Mortar Content (α):
Full registration of specific gravity (kg/dm³), loose bulk unit weight (kg/dm³), field surface moisture (h%), and unit costs ($/50 kg bag, $/t, $/kg, or $/m³) for Portland cement, natural sand, and coarse aggregates (Gravel 1 and Gravel 2). Includes an experimental binary coarse aggregate granular packing table (0% to 100% in 10% steps per ABNT NBR 7211:2022 / NBR NM 45) and laboratory simulation of the progressive dry mortar content sequence (α = (1 + a)/(1 + m) from 40% to 68% in 2% steps) holding coarse aggregate mass constant inside the mixer.

3. Analytical Regression Engine (Abrams, Lyse, and Molinari) & IPT 4-Quadrant Diagram:
Input of the 3 auxiliary laboratory trial mixes (Rich, Pilot/Intermediate, and Lean) tested at the target slump, simultaneous IEEE 754 double-precision ordinary least squares (OLS) fitting of Abrams' Exponential Law (fcj = k1 / k2^(w/c)), quadratic polynomial regression, Lyse's Linear Law (m = k3 + k4 * (w/c)), and Molinari's Hyperbolic Law (C = 1000 / (1/γc + a/γa + p/γb + w/c)), with interactive Qt Charts rendering of the 4-Quadrant Dosage Diagram, enlarged individual zoom quadrants, and the 5th Unified Abrams/Lyse/Molinari (IPT) Chart supporting high-DPI image export (PNG and PDF).

4. Final Proportioning, Moisture Correction, Cost Analysis, Bilingual Reports & CLI:
Exact computation of dry unit mix proportions (1 : a : p : w/c) and surface-moisture-corrected proportions (strictly deducting free moisture carried by wet aggregates), material consumptions per 1 m³ of compacted concrete, exact laboratory mixer batch weights, jobsite volume-box sizing (padiolas, 35 cm x 45 cm base per 50 kg cement bag), comparative cost evaluation between analytical equations and manual graphical readings (with dynamic $ / R$ currency symbol switching), 6 pre-configured engineering scenarios (including the audited UFPR TC031 spreadsheet in Scenario 5), instant runtime localization (en_US and pt_BR under Settings > Language), Modeless auxiliary dialogs with independent Z-Order, HTML5/Markdown/A4 PDF report export, and full headless automation via concrete-mix-designer-cli.exe.
